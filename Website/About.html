History:

Empowering the Nation was founded in 2022 by Precious Radebe, a passionate community advocate with a strong desire to uplift and empower individuals who have historically been overlooked. Inspired by her own family’s experiences—particularly her parents and elderly relatives who never had the opportunity to gain formal education or upskill—Precious created this initiative to help people like them break the cycle of limited opportunity.
The organization was born out of a deep need to provide accessible, practical, and life-changing training for domestic workers and gardeners in Johannesburg. Since its inception, Empowering the Nation has successfully trained hundreds of individuals, equipping them with skills that not only improve their employability but also boost their confidence, independence, and income-earning potential.
Today, the organization continues to grow, offering both short courses and long-term programmes that open doors to better jobs and even entrepreneurship. What started as a heartfelt mission has become a powerful movement towards social and economic empowerment.

About us:

Empowering the Nation is a community-focused skills training initiative based in Johannesburg, South Africa. We specialize in providing high-quality training for domestic workers, gardeners, and others from similar backgrounds who are seeking to improve their skillset, enhance their marketability, and increase their earning potential.
Our organization offers two main types of training programmes:

  • Six-Month Learnerships – in-depth training over 6 Months
  • Short Skills Programmes – compact and intensive six-week courses

The training is practical, easy to follow, and designed to fit into the lives of working adults. Whether someone is looking to offer more services to their employer, find better work opportunities, or even start their own small business, Empowering the Nation is here to support that journey.
We believe that education and skills development are not luxuries—they are rights. And with the right support, anyone can improve their future.

Vision:

Our vision is to create a society where every domestic worker, gardener, and community member has access to skills training that leads to dignity, independence, and financial empowerment.
We envision a South Africa where:

  • Workers are respected for their skill and knowledge
  • Households benefit from a more skilled workforce
  • Entrepreneurship is a viable and supported path for all
  • Communities uplift themselves through education and support

Through our work, we aim to break down barriers and build bridges—one course, one individual, one success story at a time.

Mission:

Our mission is to empower and uplift underserved individuals—particularly domestic workers and gardeners—by offering affordable, accessible, and high-quality training programmes that equip them with practical, income-generating skills.
We are committed to:

  • Providing training that is relevant, up-to-date, and focused on real-world application
  • Ensuring all our learners leave with skills they can immediately use to better their lives
  • Partnering with employers, families, and communities to support lifelong learning
  • Promoting entrepreneurship and self-employment as a path to financial freedom

Our mission is not just to educate, but to transform lives.

What we offer:

At Empowering the Nation, we offer more than just training—we offer hope, opportunity, and a path to a better life.
Our programmes are designed to be flexible, supportive, and focused on real-life skills that make a tangible difference. We offer:
Six-Month Learnership Programmes: These in-depth, 12-week courses offer comprehensive training in areas that are highly valuable to both employers and self-employed professionals.
Six-Week Short Courses: Perfect for those who want to quickly gain a new skill or enhance their existing abilities in a focused area.
Affordable Fees: Our courses are priced to be accessible, with discounts for multiple course selections.
Empowerment Through Education: Whether you’re an employer looking to upskill your domestic staff, or an individual wanting to improve your career prospects, our programmes are tailored to meet your needs.
Supportive Learning Environment: We offer a welcoming and encouraging space where learners feel respected, capable, and motivated.
