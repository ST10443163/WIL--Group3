About us:

Empowering the Nation is a skills training initiative founded by Precious Radebe in 2022 to provide training to domestic workers and gardeners in Johannesburg.
The aim is to improve employability, enhance earning potential, and enable entrepreneurship.
The proposed website and mobile app will serve as a central platform for advertising the business, providing course information, receiving customer enquiries, and generating quotes.

Courses:

We offer a range of skills training programmes designed to empower and uplift domestic workers and gardeners.
Our courses are divided into two main categories:

Six-Week Short Skills Training Programmes – practical, focused short courses to quickly enhance specific skills.
Twelve-Week (Six-Month) Learnership Programmes – comprehensive, in-depth training to build broader skills and knowledge.

Discount Structure for Multiple Courses:

  • One course - No discount
  • Two courses - 5% discount
  • Three courses - 10% discount
  • More than three courses - 15% discount
Learners can choose multiple courses to broaden their skill set and benefit from our progressive discount system.
